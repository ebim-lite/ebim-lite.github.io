Why this benchmark

Physical AI learning and development for all university and industry learners

EBiM-Lite is the dual SO-101 adaptation of the EBiM (Embodied Bimanual Manipulation) benchmark. It adapts the three tracks of the full benchmark and runs them on open-source, low-cost hardware so that more university teams can take part. Teams learn in simulation, then the best policies are validated across sites and evaluated on real robots.

Full autonomy only

No teleoperation. Only full autonomy is evaluated.

3 tracks, ranked separately

Each track has its own ranking.

Sim → Real

Isaac Sim pre-selection decides who advances to real-robot evaluation.

Benchmark tasks

Three tracks

Track 1Deformable Object Manipulation

Cable Routing

Industrial scenario: path recognition → cable grasping → routing → fixation, on a 20 cm × 20 cm board.

Time limit60 min / attempt
ModeFull autonomy only
Primary metricCompletion rate
Tie-breakerCompletion time

Task flow

1Path recognition
2Cable grasping
3Routing
4Fixation

Scenario

  • Route the cable per the given configuration, fixture sequence and direction
  • Only full autonomy is evaluated: no teleoperation
  • 60-minute time limit per attempt
  • The task board is scaled down from the full EBiM version

Scoring

Completion Rate = Prefix ÷ Total Prefix = continuous correct fixtures from the start
  • Primary metric: completion rate (%)
  • Tie-breaker: completion time
Scoring example
12345678910

A board with 10 fixtures. Fixtures 1–6 are routed correctly, #7 is missed, and 8–10 are routed correctly afterwards.

Prefix = 6 → completion rate = 6 ÷ 10 = 60%. Fixtures 8–10 do not count.

Illustrative example only. Official details will be in the rulebook.

Track 2Precision Placement

Thermal Pad Pick-and-Place

Industrial electronics manufacturing: Thermal Interface Material (TIM) assembly benchmark.

Targets3 memory modules (random)
ModeFull autonomy only
Primary metricP × O × IoU
ThenRaw IoU → time

Scenario

  • Pick a thermal pad and place it on a randomly assigned target
  • Only 3 memory modules are used as candidate targets
  • Keep correct orientation: wrong orientation scores 0
  • Placement accuracy scored by IoU (0.0 – 1.0)

Scoring

Score = P × O × IoU Pick success × Orientation × IoU
  • Primary metric: P × O × IoU
  • Secondary metric: raw IoU
  • Tie-breaker: completion time
Scoring examples
POIoUScore
✓ picked✓ correct0.850.85
✓ picked✗ wrong0.900
✗ failed——0

If two teams tie on P × O × IoU, raw IoU decides, then completion time.

Illustrative example only. Official details will be in the rulebook.

Track 3Long-Horizon Embodied Manipulation

Service Robot Challenge

Full service cycle: table setup → bean recovery → cleanup, across the Kitchen, Dining and Garbage Collection areas. Objects: cup, bowl of coffee beans, spoon.

Max score10 pts, 3 stages
ModeFull autonomy only
Ranked byHighest completed stage
ThenTotal score → time

Stages & scoring (max 10 pts)

Setup · 3
Bean Recovery · 4
Cleanup · 3

Ranking priority

  1. Highest completed stage
  2. Total score
  3. Completion time
Scoring example
StageResultPoints
1 · Table SetupAll objects at their targets3
2 · Bean Recovery90% of the bean mass recovered4 × 90% = 3.6
3 · Cleanup2 of 3 objects in the sink region2
Total8.6 / 10

Teams are compared first by the highest completed stage, then by total score, then by completion time.

Illustrative example only. Official details will be in the rulebook.

Stage 1 · 3 pts

Table Setup

  • Transport cup, bowl (with coffee beans) and spoon from Kitchen to Dining Area
  • Three randomly assigned targets: one for the cup, one adjacent to the robot head for bowl and spoon
  • Stage complete when every object sits at its assigned location
Stage 2 · 4 pts

Bean Recovery

  • Transfer the coffee beans from the bowl into the recycling container in the Garbage Collection Area
  • Container sits on a scale; Recovery Ratio = recovered mass ÷ original mass
  • Points awarded proportionally (100% → full points, 90% → 90%, 80% → 80%)
Stage 3 · 3 pts

Cleanup

  • Return cup, bowl and spoon to the Kitchen Area
  • Place each inside the sink region: the black rectangle on the countertop
  • 1 point per object inside the sink region
Competition platform

Low-cost mobile bimanual robot

Two SO-ARM101 arms on a TurtleBot 4. Open-source hardware and software lower the barrier for university teams, and the compact footprint suits the 100 cm aisles and 30 cm tables of the EBiM-Lite arena.

End effectorsParallel grippers
Arm sensingWrist cameras
Mobile baseTurtleBot 4 (iRobot Create 3)
Base sensingOAK-D camera + lidar
SimulationIsaac Sim / Isaac Lab
Onboard computeJetson AGX Orin (TBC)
Rules3 tracks adapted from full EBiM
