[[Physical AI learning and development for all university and industry learners::讓所有大學與產業學習者都能投入 Physical AI 的學習與開發]]

[[EBiM-Lite is the dual SO-101 adaptation of the EBiM (Embodied Bimanual Manipulation) benchmark. It adapts the three tracks of the full benchmark and runs them on open-source, low-cost hardware so that more university teams can take part. Teams learn in simulation, then the best policies are validated across sites and evaluated on real robots.::EBiM-Lite 是 EBiM（具身雙臂操作）Benchmark 的雙 SO-101 版本。它改編完整版的三個賽道， 並改用開源低成本硬體，讓更多大學團隊能參加。隊伍先在模擬中學習，表現最好的策略再經跨站驗證，最後在真實機器人上評測。]]

[[Benchmark tasks::賽道任務]]

[[Three tracks::三個賽道]]

Track 1[[Deformable Object Manipulation::可變形物體操作]]

[[Cable Routing::線纜佈線]]

[[Industrial scenario: path recognition → cable grasping → routing → fixation, on a 20 cm × 20 cm board.::工業情境：路徑辨識 → 線纜抓取 → 佈線 → 固定，於 20 cm × 20 cm 的板上進行。]]

[[Time limit::時間限制]][[60 min / attempt::每次 60 分鐘]]
[[Mode::模式]][[Full autonomy only::僅限全自主]]
[[Primary metric::主要指標]][[Completion rate::完成率]]
[[Tie-breaker::同分比較]][[Completion time::完成時間]]

[[Task flow::任務流程]]

1[[Path recognition::路徑辨識]]
2[[Cable grasping::線纜抓取]]
3[[Routing::佈線]]
4[[Fixation::固定]]

[[Scenario::任務情境]]

  • [[Route the cable per the given configuration, fixture sequence and direction::依指定的配置、夾具順序與方向完成線纜佈線]]
  • [[Only full autonomy is evaluated: no teleoperation::只評全自主運作，不可遙控操作]]
  • [[60-minute time limit per attempt::每次嘗試限時 60 分鐘]]
  • [[The task board is scaled down from the full EBiM version::任務板由完整版 EBiM 縮小]]

[[Scoring::計分方式]]

[[Completion Rate::完成率]] = Prefix ÷ Total [[Prefix = continuous correct fixtures from the start::Prefix = 從起點開始連續正確的夾具數]]
  • [[Primary metric::主要指標]][[: ::：]][[completion rate (%)::完成率（%）]]
  • [[Tie-breaker::同分比較]][[: ::：]][[completion time::完成時間]]
[[Scoring example::計分範例]]
12345678910

[[A board with 10 fixtures. Fixtures 1–6 are routed correctly, #7 is missed, and 8–10 are routed correctly afterwards.::假設板上有 10 個夾具：第 1–6 個正確，第 7 個錯誤，之後第 8–10 個雖然正確。]]

Prefix = 6 → [[completion rate::完成率]] = 6 ÷ 10 = 60%[[. Fixtures 8–10 do not count.::，第 8–10 個不計分。]]

[[Illustrative example only. Official details will be in the rulebook.::僅為示意範例，正式細節以規則手冊為準。]]

Track 2[[Precision Placement::精密放置]]

[[Thermal Pad Pick-and-Place::導熱墊取放]]

[[Industrial electronics manufacturing: Thermal Interface Material (TIM) assembly benchmark.::工業電子製造情境：導熱介面材料（TIM）組裝 Benchmark。]]

[[Targets::目標]][[3 memory modules (random)::3 條記憶體模組（隨機）]]
[[Mode::模式]][[Full autonomy only::僅限全自主]]
[[Primary metric::主要指標]]P × O × IoU
[[Then::其次]][[Raw IoU → time::原始 IoU → 時間]]

[[Scenario::任務情境]]

  • [[Pick a thermal pad and place it on a randomly assigned target::夾取一片導熱墊，放到隨機指定的目標上]]
  • [[Only 3 memory modules are used as candidate targets::候選目標只有 3 條記憶體模組]]
  • [[Keep correct orientation: wrong orientation scores 0::必須保持正確方向：方向錯誤即得 0 分]]
  • [[Placement accuracy scored by IoU (0.0 – 1.0)::放置精度以 IoU（0.0 – 1.0）計分]]

[[Scoring::計分方式]]

[[Score::分數]] = P × O × IoU [[Pick success × Orientation × IoU::夾取成功 × 方向正確 × IoU]]
  • [[Primary metric::主要指標]][[: ::：]]P × O × IoU
  • [[Secondary metric::次要指標]][[: ::：]][[raw IoU::原始 IoU]]
  • [[Tie-breaker::同分比較]][[: ::：]][[completion time::完成時間]]
[[Scoring examples::計分範例]]
POIoU[[Score::分數]]
[[✓ picked::✓ 夾取成功]][[✓ correct::✓ 方向正確]]0.850.85
[[✓ picked::✓ 夾取成功]][[✗ wrong::✗ 方向錯誤]]0.900
[[✗ failed::✗ 夾取失敗]]——0

[[If two teams tie on P × O × IoU, raw IoU decides, then completion time.::若兩隊 P × O × IoU 同分，先比原始 IoU，再比完成時間。]]

[[Illustrative example only. Official details will be in the rulebook.::僅為示意範例，正式細節以規則手冊為準。]]

Track 3[[Long-Horizon Embodied Manipulation::長程具身操作]]

[[Service Robot Challenge::服務機器人挑戰]]

[[Full service cycle: table setup → bean recovery → cleanup, across the Kitchen, Dining and Garbage Collection areas. Objects: cup, bowl of coffee beans, spoon.::完整服務流程：布置餐桌 → 回收咖啡豆 → 清理，橫跨廚房區、用餐區與垃圾回收區。物品：杯子、一碗咖啡豆、湯匙。]]

[[Max score::滿分]][[10 pts, 3 stages::10 分，3 階段]]
[[Mode::模式]][[Full autonomy only::僅限全自主]]
[[Ranked by::排名依據]][[Highest completed stage::完成的最高階段]]
[[Then::其次]][[Total score → time::總分 → 時間]]

[[Stages & scoring (max 10 pts)::階段與配分（滿分 10 分）]]

[[Setup · 3::布置 · 3]]
[[Bean Recovery · 4::回收咖啡豆 · 4]]
[[Cleanup · 3::清理 · 3]]

[[Ranking priority::排名順序]]

  1. [[Highest completed stage::完成的最高階段]]
  2. [[Total score::總分]]
  3. [[Completion time::完成時間]]
[[Scoring example::計分範例]]
[[Stage::階段]][[Result::結果]][[Points::得分]]
[[1 · Table Setup::1 · 布置餐桌]][[All objects at their targets::所有物品都到達目標]]3
[[2 · Bean Recovery::2 · 回收咖啡豆]][[90% of the bean mass recovered::回收 90% 的咖啡豆重量]]4 × 90% = 3.6
[[3 · Cleanup::3 · 清理]][[2 of 3 objects in the sink region::3 件中有 2 件放進水槽區域]]2
[[Total::總分]]8.6 / 10

[[Teams are compared first by the highest completed stage, then by total score, then by completion time.::排名時先比完成的最高階段，再比總分，最後比完成時間。]]

[[Illustrative example only. Official details will be in the rulebook.::僅為示意範例，正式細節以規則手冊為準。]]

[[Stage 1 · 3 pts::Stage 1 · 3 分]]

[[Table Setup::布置餐桌]]

  • [[Transport cup, bowl (with coffee beans) and spoon from Kitchen to Dining Area::把杯子、碗（裝有咖啡豆）與湯匙從廚房區送到用餐區]]
  • [[Three randomly assigned targets: one for the cup, one adjacent to the robot head for bowl and spoon::三個隨機指定的目標：一個放杯子，碗與湯匙放在機器人頭部旁]]
  • [[Stage complete when every object sits at its assigned location::所有物品都到達指定位置即完成此階段]]
[[Stage 2 · 4 pts::Stage 2 · 4 分]]

[[Bean Recovery::回收咖啡豆]]

  • [[Transfer the coffee beans from the bowl into the recycling container in the Garbage Collection Area::把碗中的咖啡豆倒入垃圾回收區的回收容器]]
  • [[Container sits on a scale; Recovery Ratio = recovered mass ÷ original mass::容器放在秤上；回收率 = 回收重量 ÷ 原始重量]]
  • [[Points awarded proportionally (100% → full points, 90% → 90%, 80% → 80%)::依比例給分（100% → 滿分、90% → 90%、80% → 80%）]]
[[Stage 3 · 3 pts::Stage 3 · 3 分]]

[[Cleanup::清理]]

  • [[Return cup, bowl and spoon to the Kitchen Area::把杯子、碗與湯匙送回廚房區]]
  • [[Place each inside the sink region: the black rectangle on the countertop::逐一放進水槽區域：流理台上的黑色矩形]]
  • [[1 point per object inside the sink region::每放進一件得 1 分]]
