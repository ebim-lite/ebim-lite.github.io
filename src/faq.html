[[Getting started::入門]]

[[What is EBiM-Lite?::什麼是 EBiM-Lite？]]
[[EBiM-Lite is the dual SO-101 adaptation of the EBiM (Embodied Bimanual Manipulation) benchmark. It uses two SO-ARM101 arms on a TurtleBot 4 and adapts the three tracks of the full benchmark. See EBiM vs EBiM-Lite.::EBiM-Lite 是 EBiM（具身雙臂操作）Benchmark 的雙 SO-101 版本，使用 TurtleBot 4 搭載兩支 SO-ARM101 手臂，並改編完整版的三個賽道。詳見 EBiM 與 Lite 比較。]]
[[Who can participate?::誰可以參加？]]
[[University and industry learners. Detailed eligibility rules will be published in the rulebook.::大學與產業學習者。詳細資格規定將於規則手冊公布。]]
[[Do I need my own robot?::需要自備機器人嗎？]]
[[No. Stage I runs in simulation (Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab). Selected Stage II teams get a DGX Spark or RTX machine on loan, and a limited number of SO-101 arms are provided.::不需要。Stage I 在模擬中進行（Isaac Sim／Isaac Lab）。入選 Stage II 的隊伍可借用 DGX Spark 或 RTX 機器，並提供數量有限的 SO-101 機械臂。]]
[[Where can I learn the required skills?::要去哪裡學需要的技能？]]
[[See the recommended NVIDIA DLI courses in Learning & Compute.::請見學習與算力頁面的 NVIDIA DLI 課程。]]

[[The competition::競賽]]

[[Is teleoperation allowed?::可以遙控操作嗎？]]
[[No. Only full autonomy is evaluated.::不行。只評全自主運作。]]
[[How are ties broken?::同分時怎麼比？]]
[[Completion time is the tie-breaker in all tracks. In Track 3, ranking uses the highest completed stage first, then total score, then completion time.::所有賽道都以完成時間作為同分比較。Track 3 會先比完成的最高階段，再比總分，最後比完成時間。]]
[[Can my team enter more than one track?::一隊可以報名多個賽道嗎？]]
[[Each track is ranked separately. Rules for entering several tracks will be published in the rulebook.::每個賽道分開排名。報名多個賽道的規定將於規則手冊公布。]]
[[How do we submit?::要怎麼提交？]]
[[The submission format will be announced before the simulation pre-selection (Feb 25, 2027). See Submission.::提交格式將於模擬初選（2027/02/25）前公布。詳見提交方式。]]
[[Where is the final?::決賽在哪裡？]]
[[At GTC Taipei in mid-May 2027. The top 3 teams receive prizes.::2027 年 5 月中於 GTC Taipei 舉行，前三名隊伍獲獎。]]

[[Compute::算力]]

[[What compute do teams get?::隊伍有哪些算力資源？]]
[[Stage I: L40 120 hr, A100 100 hr, H100 50 hr, plus Brev credits. Stage II: an RTX / DGX Spark for 40 days plus the same GPU hours. See Computing resources.::Stage I：L40 120 小時、A100 100 小時、H100 50 小時，加上 Brev 額度。Stage II：RTX／DGX Spark 40 天，加上相同的 GPU 時數。詳見算力資源。]]
