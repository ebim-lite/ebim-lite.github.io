Getting started

What is EBiM-Lite?
EBiM-Lite is the low-cost adaptation of the EBiM (Embodied Bimanual Manipulation) benchmark. It uses two SO-ARM101 arms on a TurtleBot 4, with the same three tracks and scoring rules as the full benchmark.
Who can participate?
The competition is open to university and industry learners. We expect 10–15 universities, 25+ teams and 200+ participants in Stage I.
Do I need my own robot?
No. Stage I runs entirely in simulation (Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab). Teams selected for Stage II receive SO-101 arms, a TurtleBot 4 base and a loaned DGX Spark or RTX workstation.
Where can I learn the required skills?
See Learning & Compute for the recommended NVIDIA DLI courses. Most of them are free and self-paced.

The competition

Can my team enter more than one track?
Each track is ranked separately. Rules for entering more than one track will be in the rulebook.
Is teleoperation allowed?
No. Only full autonomy is evaluated in every track.
How are ties broken?
Completion time is the tie-breaker in all tracks. In Track 3, ranking first uses the highest completed stage, then total score, then completion time.
Where is the final?
The real-robot final takes place at GTC Taipei in mid-May 2027. The top 3 teams receive prizes.

Compute

What compute do teams get?
Stage I teams receive L40 (120 hr), A100 (100 hr) and H100 (50 hr) GPU time plus Brev credits. Stage II teams additionally receive a 40-day DGX Spark / RTX workstation loan. See Computing resources.
